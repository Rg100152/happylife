# ♥ Happy Life

**A calm, hopeful mental health support & helpline web experience.**

Happy Life is a simple, accessible website designed to help people in emotional distress. It provides immediate crisis resources, gentle self-check tools, calming exercises, and trusted information — without judgment and without requiring an account.

> **You are not alone. Help is available right now.**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Always-visible Crisis Banner**  
  Quick access to emergency helplines (988 in the US, international directories, Crisis Text Line, and more).

- **Mood Check-in**  
  Simple 5-level emoji mood selector with supportive, non-judgmental feedback.

- **Calm Tools** (no login required)
  - Guided breathing exercise with animated visual
  - Private journal (saved only in the browser)
  - 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise

- **Helpline Directory**  
  Clear cards for major crisis services with direct call / text links.

- **Trusted Resources**  
  Links to NIMH, IASP, MentalHealth.gov, WHO, and other reputable organizations.

- **Clean & Accessible Design**  
  Soft blue-green palette, large readable text, mobile-first, and reduced visual stress.

## 🚀 Live Demo / How to Use

1. Open `happy-life.html` in any modern browser

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

Happy Life provides **information and self-help tools only**.  
It is **not** a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

**If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself:**
- Call emergency services immediately
- Contact a crisis helpline (e.g. **988** in the United States)
- Visit [IASP – Find a Helpline](https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/) for local resources
